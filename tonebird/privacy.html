Privacy Policy

Tonebird for iPhone and iPad (“the app”), by Sergei Lavrinenko.
Last updated: November 10, 2026.

Summary

The app collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours for anything to be sent to. The app makes no network requests at all. What you do in it stays on your device.

Data on your device

Your test scores, course progress, the things you keep getting wrong, your streak and your settings are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

The microphone

The singing exercises listen to the microphone so the app can tell you which note you sang and how far off you are. The microphone is read only while one of those exercises is on screen. The sound is analysed for pitch as it arrives and is never written to disk, never kept after the exercise, and never sent anywhere. No recording is made.

iOS asks your permission the first time, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Tonebird › Microphone. Every other part of the app works without it.

Purchases

Tonebird Pro is a subscription sold through Apple’s App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we do not receive or see your payment details. The app asks the App Store only whether a subscription is active on your Apple Account. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
