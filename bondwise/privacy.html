Privacy Policy

Bondwise for iPhone (“the app”), by Sergei Lavrinenko.
Last updated: November 2, 2026.

Summary

There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Your test answers, the people you add and your results stay on your phone. The only feature that sends anything off your phone is the situation advisor, a paid feature you must agree to first.

Data on your phone

Your test answers, your results, the names and answers you enter about other people, and your advisor history are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. You can erase them in Settings › Delete all my data, or by deleting the app.

The situation advisor

When you ask the advisor a question, the app sends our server:

  • the text you wrote;
  • both attachment results (style and levels) and the type of relationship, with the app’s related guidance text;
  • your App Store subscription’s signed transaction, to confirm the subscription is active.

The name of the person you are asking about is replaced with “Person A” before sending, and the name is replaced back on your phone when the answer arrives. Other names or personal details you type are sent as written, so please leave out anything you would rather not share. What you write may reveal sensitive things about you or someone else, such as health or relationships. Nothing is sent unless you first agree in the app.

Our server, which runs on Cloudflare, passes your text to Google’s Gemini AI model to write the answer, and does not store your text or the answer. Google may log prompts and responses for a limited time solely to detect abuse, and does not use them to improve its products. See the Gemini API terms and the Google Privacy Policy. As with any internet service, Cloudflare handles your IP address to deliver the request; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

To enforce the daily question limit, our server keeps a count per day against a one-way hash of your transaction ID. It contains no text, names or results, and is deleted after about 30 days.

If you describe a crisis or a threat to someone’s safety, the app shows safety information and helplines rather than regular advice. That reply is still written through the same service, and we do not record that it happened.

Legal basis (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

We process advisor data based on your explicit consent, given in the app, and to provide the service you subscribed to. Stop using the advisor at any time to stop sending data. You may have rights to access, erase or object, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we keep no text and no account, we usually cannot link stored counters back to you; write to us and we will help as far as we can.

Purchases

Bondwise Plus is sold through Apple’s App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Age

Bondwise is for adults aged 18 and over.

Not a medical service

Bondwise offers reflection, not diagnosis or therapy.
