Contact

Questions, bugs or feedback: ceofarming@gmail.com.

Subscription

Bondwise Plus is billed by Apple. To cancel or change it, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore it on a new phone, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your answers and the people you add stay on your phone. To erase them, open Settings in the app and tap Delete all my data. See the Privacy Policy.

If you are in crisis

Bondwise is not a medical service and can’t help in an emergency. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number. You can find a helpline near you at findahelpline.com.
