The full version

The full version is a one-time purchase, billed by Apple. It is not a subscription and nothing renews. To get it back on a new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your answers and the people you add stay on your phone. To erase them, open Settings in the app and tap Delete all my data. Because nothing is stored outside your phone, your answers do not move to a new phone by themselves. See the Privacy Policy.

About the results

The four DISC styles are tendencies, not fixed types, and they shift with the situation. The questions are our own and have not been normed on a sample, so treat the result as a starting point for reflection. It is not a measure of ability and not a basis for hiring decisions. The app is an independent product and is not affiliated with any publisher of DISC assessments.
