Privacy Policy

DISC Test for iPhone (“the app”), by Sergei Lavrinenko.
Last updated: November 24, 2026.

Summary

The app collects nothing. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or AI, and the app has no server. Your test answers, your results and the people you add stay on your phone.

Data on your phone

Your test answers, your results, and the names and answers you enter about other people are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them. You can erase them in Settings › Delete all my data, or by deleting the app.

Other people

When you answer questions about someone else, those answers describe how you see that person. They stay on your phone like everything else. A name or nickname is enough; the app never asks for contact details.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase sold through Apple’s App Store. It is not a subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details or your identity. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Not an assessment for hiring, and not a medical service

The app is a tool for reflection. It is not a measure of ability, not a basis for hiring or other employment decisions, and not a diagnosis.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
