Tunebird

A clear, accurate tuner for string instruments: guitar (6, 7 and 8 strings), bass, ukulele, dombra, balalaika, domra, mandolin, banjo, violin, viola, cello, double bass, gusli, sitar, and a chromatic mode for any note. The whole screen turns blue when a string is too low, orange when it is too high and green when it is in tune, so you can read it from a distance.

Frequently asked questions

The tuner does not react.
Allow microphone access in Settings › Tunebird › Microphone. Pluck one string at a time, close to the phone, in a quiet room.

How do I tune by ear?
Tap a string button at the bottom to hear its note. Tap it again to switch back to automatic string detection.

What is free and what is in the full version?
All instruments and all built-in tunings are free. The full version is a one-time purchase that removes ads and adds your own tunings (any number of strings, any notes) and a custom reference pitch (A4 from 415 to 466 Hz).

I bought the full version on another device.
Open the full version screen and tap Restore purchase.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?
Where the law requires it, the settings screen (⚙ at the bottom) has Ad privacy settings. See the privacy policy.
