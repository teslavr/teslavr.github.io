Privacy Policy

Tunebird for iPhone (“the app”), by Sergei Lavrinenko.
Last updated: November 26, 2026.

Summary

The app has no accounts and we do not run any servers that collect your data. The microphone is used only to hear your instrument; sound is analysed on your device and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The free version shows a small ad banner provided by Google AdMob; Google may collect device data for advertising as described below. With the full version there are no ads.

Microphone

The app listens to the microphone only while the tuner screen is open, to detect the pitch of your string. The audio is processed in memory on your device and discarded immediately. It is never recorded, saved or transmitted.

Your settings

Your chosen instrument and tuning, your own tunings and the reference pitch are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google’s SDK may collect information such as your device’s advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app: tap ⚙ at the bottom of the screen, then Ad privacy settings.

On iOS, the app asks for permission to track only to show more relevant ads. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google’s use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Buying the full version removes ads and all ad-related data collection.

Purchases

The full version is sold through Apple’s App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we do not receive your payment details. The app asks the App Store only whether the full version has been purchased on your Apple Account. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
