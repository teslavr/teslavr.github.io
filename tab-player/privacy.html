Privacy Policy

Easy GP Tabs Player for iPhone and iPad (“the app”), by Sergei Lavrinenko.
Last updated: October 27, 2026.

Summary

The app has no accounts and we do not run any servers that collect your data. Your tabs never leave your device. The free version shows a small ad banner provided by Google AdMob; Google may collect device data for advertising as described below. With Pro there are no ads and the app collects nothing.

Your files and settings

Tabs you add are copied into the app’s own storage on your device. Your library and practice settings (speed, shown track, muted tracks and so on) are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. We never upload or see your files.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google’s SDK may collect information such as your device’s advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app: in the library, tap ⋯, then Privacy Settings.

On iOS, the app asks for permission to track only to show more relevant ads. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google’s use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Buying Pro removes ads and all ad-related data collection.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we do not receive your payment details. The app asks the App Store only whether Pro has been purchased on your Apple Account. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Audio

The app plays sound but never records or uses the microphone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
